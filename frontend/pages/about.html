Our Story

Born From a Love
of the Hive

What began as a backyard beekeeping experiment became a dedication to sharing the world's finest, most honest honey.

🐝
The Beginning

A Single Hive in 2014

HoneyGold was founded by two beekeepers who were frustrated by what passed for "honey" on supermarket shelves. Ultra-filtered, heated, blended — stripped of everything that makes honey extraordinary.

They started with one hive in rural Portugal, selling jars at local markets. Word spread. Demand grew. Today we partner with 28 apiaries across 9 countries — but the mission hasn't changed: real honey for real people.

What We Stand For

Our Values

🧪
Radical Transparency

Every batch has a QR code linking to lab results, apiary location, and bloom season data. No secrets.

🐝
Bee Welfare First

We never over-harvest. Bees keep more than half of what they produce. A healthy hive means exceptional honey.

🤝
Fair Beekeeper Pay

Our partner beekeepers earn 3× the commodity rate. Good honey requires generous investment in people.

♻️
Zero Waste Packaging

Glass jars, natural wax seals, and recycled cardboard. We obsess over our environmental footprint.

🌡️
Raw Integrity

Honey that has been heated above 95°F loses enzymes and aroma. We cold-process, always. Period.

🌍
Global, Local Heart

We source from 9 countries but every beekeeper is known by name. Scale doesn't have to mean distance.

Milestones

A Decade of Honey

2014

First Hive

Elena and James set up their first hive in rural Portugal, start selling at Cascais farmers market.

2016

HoneyGold Founded

Company officially registered. First website launched, 3 products, 120 jars in the first month.

2018

International Sourcing

Partnerships with apiaries in New Zealand, Morocco, and Greece. Manuka Reserve launched to instant acclaim.

2021

50,000 Customers

Crossed the 50k customer mark. Launched the Beekeeper Partnership Fund, supporting 8 small apiaries.

2024

Today & Beyond

28 apiaries, 12 varieties, and still growing. Sophie joins to expand the infused line. The hive lives on.

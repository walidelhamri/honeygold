Common Questions

How do I know your honey is truly raw?

Every batch is third-party lab tested for enzyme activity (diastase), HMF levels, and moisture content. Scan the QR code on your jar to see your batch's certificate of analysis.

My honey has crystallized — is it spoiled?

Not at all! Crystallization is a natural sign of raw, unprocessed honey. To re-liquefy, place the jar in warm water (never microwave). It's a quality indicator, not a flaw.

Do you offer wholesale or bulk orders?

Yes. We work with restaurants, specialty retailers, and corporate gifting programs. Contact us with your requirements for pricing and minimum order quantities.

What is your shipping and return policy?

We ship worldwide. Free shipping on orders over $50 within the US. International rates calculated at checkout. Returns accepted within 30 days for unopened products.

Is honey safe for children under 1?

No. Honey of any kind — raw or pasteurized — should not be given to infants under 12 months due to the risk of infant botulism. Consult your pediatrician for guidance.

How long does honey last?

Properly stored honey has an essentially indefinite shelf life. Keep it at room temperature, away from direct sunlight, and sealed. Ancient honey found in Egyptian tombs was still edible.
